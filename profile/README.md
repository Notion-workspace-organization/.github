# Notion

## About Notion

Notion is a flexible productivity workspace for notes, tasks, wikis, and databases. Notion combines block-based editing with relational databases to handle personal planning, team collaboration, and content libraries. Notion replaces multiple tools with one connected system. Notion works for students, creators, and remote teams who need structure without rigidity. Notion keeps your knowledge searchable and your projects visible. Notion adapts to journaling, sprint tracking, or document storage with the same core building blocks. Notion offers a clean interface where each page becomes a dashboard. Notion supports linked databases, embedded media, and real-time syncing across devices. Notion remains the central place for your workflows, reference materials, and action lists.

## Key Features of Notion

- **Block-based Editor** – Notion pages are built from blocks: text, headings, bullets, toggles, images, video, code, and embeds. Notion blocks can be dragged, duplicated, or converted on the fly.
- **Databases with Views** – Notion databases display as tables, boards, lists, galleries, or calendars. Notion views help you track tasks, leads, content, or goals from different angles.
- **Linked Relations** – Notion connects entries through relation and rollup properties. Notion shows aggregated data across projects, clients, or departments without duplication.
- **Collaboration Tools** – Notion supports comments, mentions, and real-time editing. Notion works for team reviews, meeting follow-ups, and shared documentation.
- **Cross-Platform Sync** – Notion syncs across web, mobile, and desktop environments. Notion keeps your workspace updated wherever you make changes.

## Quick Start with Notion

1. **Open Notion** – Launch Notion and sign in to your workspace. Notion shows your sidebar with pages and shared spaces.
2. **Create a page** – Click "New page" in Notion and choose a blank canvas or template. Notion prompts you to add a title and first block.
3. **Add content blocks** – Type "/" in Notion to insert blocks: headings, lists, database views, or embeds. Notion builds your page as you add blocks.
4. **Set up a database** – Use Notion's table or board view to track projects, contacts, or reading lists. Notion lets you define properties like text, number, date, or select.
5. **Link and relate** – In Notion, add relation properties to connect database entries. Notion shows linked items and rollup calculations automatically.
6. **Share and collaborate** – Invite team members to your Notion page with view or edit permissions. Notion updates instantly for all participants.
